MNC Hiring

MNC Hiring Process

By TalentBuddy Team  ·  Updated June 2026  ·  8 min read

MNC Jobs India have become a significant part of the country's economy, with many multinational companies setting up their operations in cities like Bangalore, Hyderabad, Mumbai, Chennai, and Pune. The MNC hiring process in India is quite complex, and job seekers often find it challenging to navigate. Industry estimates suggest that around 70% of job seekers in India are looking for MNC jobs, and this trend is expected to continue in 2026.

MNC Jobs India: Understanding the Hiring Process

The hiring process for MNC jobs in India typically involves several stages, including resume screening, phone interviews, and face-to-face interviews. Job seekers can increase their chances of getting hired by MNCs in India by creating an ATS-optimised CV, which can help them get past the initial screening stage. Studies indicate that around 40% of resumes are rejected by applicant tracking systems (ATS) due to formatting issues or lack of relevant keywords.

For example, a job seeker can use online resources like Naukri or LinkedIn India to create a professional profile and increase their visibility to recruiters. Many Indian companies like TCS, Infosys, and Wipro use these platforms to find potential candidates. Additionally, job seekers can also use these platforms to network with professionals in their industry and get referrals.

Benefits of Working for MNCs in India

Working for MNCs in India can provide job seekers with a range of benefits, including competitive salaries, opportunities for career growth, and exposure to global best practices. Many MNCs in India offer their employees training and development programs, which can help them enhance their skills and knowledge. For instance, companies like Accenture and Flipkart provide their employees with opportunities to work on complex projects and develop their problem-solving skills.

Furthermore, MNCs in India often have a diverse and inclusive work culture, which can provide job seekers with a chance to work with people from different backgrounds and cultures. This can be a great way for job seekers to develop their communication and teamwork skills. According to Naukri's 2026 hiring report, around 60% of job seekers in India prefer to work for MNCs due to their reputation and work culture.

MNC Jobs India: How to Increase Your Chances of Getting Hired

To increase their chances of getting hired by MNCs in India, job seekers need to have a clear understanding of the hiring process and the skills required by the company. They can do this by researching the company's website, social media, and reviews from current and former employees. Industry estimates suggest that around 80% of job seekers in India research the company's culture and values before applying for a job.

Job seekers can also increase their chances of getting hired by MNCs in India by building a strong professional network. They can attend job fairs, industry events, and networking sessions to meet people in their industry and learn about job opportunities. For example, companies like Zomato and Flipkart often participate in job fairs and networking events to find potential candidates.

Pro tip: job seekers can increase their chances of getting hired by MNCs in India by tailoring their resume and cover letter to the specific job requirements. They can use keywords from the job description and highlight their relevant skills and experience.

Common Mistakes to Avoid in the MNC Hiring Process

There are several common mistakes that job seekers make during the MNC hiring process in India, which can reduce their chances of getting hired. One of the most common mistakes is not customizing their resume and cover letter to the specific job requirements. Studies indicate that around 50% of job seekers in India use a generic resume and cover letter for all job applications.

Another common mistake is not preparing for the interview. Job seekers should research the company, practice their responses to common interview questions, and prepare any materials they may need, such as references or portfolio samples. For example, job seekers can use online resources like ATS resume rejection to learn how to create an ATS-friendly resume.
