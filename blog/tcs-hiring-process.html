TCS Hiring Process 2026

By TalentBuddy Team  ·  Updated June 2026  ·  5 min read

The TCS hiring process is one of the most competitive and sought-after in India, with millions of candidates applying every year. Understanding the TCS hiring process can give you an edge over others, and increase your chances of getting selected. The primary keyword to focus on here is the "TCS hiring process", which involves a series of steps that candidates must go through to get hired by the company.

Industry estimates suggest that over 3.5 million students graduate in India every year, and a significant number of them apply for jobs at top IT companies like TCS, Infosys, and Wipro. To stand out from the crowd, it's essential to have a solid understanding of the TCS hiring process and what the company looks for in a candidate.

TCS Hiring Process Overview

The TCS hiring process typically involves a combination of online assessments, technical interviews, and HR interviews. Candidates who clear all the rounds are then offered a job at one of the TCS offices in cities like Bangalore, Hyderabad, Mumbai, Chennai, or Pune. According to studies, the average salary for a fresher at TCS is around Rs 3.5 lakhs per annum, which is competitive with other top IT companies in India.

Candidates can apply for TCS jobs through various channels, including the company website, Naukri, and LinkedIn India. However, many candidates face rejection due to ATS resume rejection, which can be avoided by using the right keywords and formatting in their resumes.

TCS Recruitment Process for Freshers

Freshers can apply for TCS jobs through the company's campus recruitment program, which is conducted in various engineering colleges across India. The selection process typically involves a written test, followed by a technical interview and an HR interview. Candidates who clear all the rounds are then offered a job at one of the TCS offices in India.

According to industry estimates, the TCS campus recruitment program selects around 20,000-30,000 candidates every year, out of a total of over 1 million applicants. This makes the selection process highly competitive, and candidates need to have a strong academic record and good technical skills to get selected.

TCS Hiring Process for Experienced Candidates

Experienced candidates can apply for TCS jobs through the company website or through recruitment agencies. The selection process typically involves a technical interview, followed by an HR interview. Candidates who clear all the rounds are then offered a job at one of the TCS offices in India.

Many experienced candidates face challenges in getting interview calls, and this can be due to various reasons, including a weak resume or a lack of networking. To increase their chances of getting selected, candidates can use strategies like how to get a job without referrals and hidden job market in India.

Pro tip: Use keywords related to the job profile in your resume and cover letter to increase your chances of getting shortlisted.

Candidates can also use online resources like why you are not getting interview calls to identify the reasons why they are not getting interview calls and work on improving their strategy. Additionally, they can use resume mistakes freshers make to avoid common mistakes in their resumes.

To increase their chances of getting selected in the TCS hiring process, candidates should focus on developing their technical skills and building a strong professional network. They should also be prepared to face challenges and rejections, and use them as opportunities to learn and improve.
